# Timeway Brand Guide v1

## Brand foundation

**Timeway makes it easy to find a time that works.**

Timeway is a globally English-first self-serve scheduling platform for independent professionals and teams. It makes the process of agreeing a meeting time simpler and more human. The identity should create **connection** first, followed by **confidence** and **relief**.

## Positioning and tone

Timeway is practical, calm, organized, warm, and dependable. Write with straightforward language that reduces effort rather than describing complexity. Lead with the human outcome and use the product utility to support it.

| Use | Preferred approach | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Product language | “Find a time that works.” | Technical scheduling jargon as the lead message |
| Calls to action | “Create your booking page”, “Share your availability” | Urgent or overly sales-led language |
| Error and status messages | Clear, reassuring, and specific | Blame, vague warnings, or bureaucratic phrasing |
| International writing | Plain globally understood English | Idioms, invented name origins, or region-specific colloquialisms |

## Approved logo system

The approved emblem is the **Effortless Path Forward** mark: a soft route moving through a stable rounded field toward a seafoam destination point. It represents reaching the right time with less friction, without using a literal clock, calendar, arrow, or human figure.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `logos/svg/timeway-primary.svg` | Primary horizontal logo for light surfaces |
| `logos/svg/timeway-reverse.svg` | Horizontal logo for dark ink surfaces |
| `logos/svg/timeway-monochrome-ink.svg` | One-colour reproduction on light surfaces |
| `logos/svg/timeway-monochrome-white.svg` | One-colour reproduction on dark surfaces |
| `icons/svg/timeway-app-icon.svg` | App and product icon |
| `icons/svg/timeway-favicon.svg` and `icons/png/favicon.ico` | Browser favicon assets |
| `source/timeway-emblem-master.svg` | Master emblem artwork |

All delivered logo SVGs use vector outlines for the wordmark, so they do not depend on a local font installation.

### Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around every logo equal to the diameter of the seafoam destination point in the emblem. Do not place text, rules, UI controls, or container edges inside this area. Use the horizontal lockup at no less than **136 px wide** in digital use. Use the standalone emblem at no less than **24 px**; below that size, use the supplied favicon file rather than redrawing the mark.

### Do not

Do not recolour the mark, replace the wordmark, add shadows or gradients, stretch or compress it, separate the destination point from the route, place it on busy imagery, or use the seafoam colour for small body text on a white or paper background.

## Application guidance

For web and product UI, create generous white or Paper surfaces with Ink as the structural colour. Use Seafoam sparingly to signal a positive destination, an available next step, or a selected moment. Keep interfaces visually calm: clear spacing, straightforward hierarchy, and only the information needed to take the next step.

The logo should appear at the beginning of a navigation environment or at meaningful trust moments, not repeatedly inside each scheduling action. Let the product’s clarity do the work.
